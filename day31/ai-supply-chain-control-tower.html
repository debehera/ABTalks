How the Control Tower works

Objective

Resolve incoming operational alerts before they expire. Every action affects your seven KPIs and your score. Survive three minutes with the strongest network you can build.

KPIs

Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, and Transportation Efficiency are health metrics (higher is better). Operating Cost trends against you when it rises. Revenue Protected accumulates from good calls. Score is your overall performance rating.

Actions

Each alert offers several responses — some are the "right" fix, others are faster but costlier, and some (Ignore / Delay) are gambles. Critical alerts that expire unresolved cause serious KPI damage.

Difficulty curve

Alerts spawn faster and stack up as the timer runs down — expect multiple simultaneous incidents in the final minute.
