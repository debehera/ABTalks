
🚀 60DayClaudeChallenge – Day 28

🏥 Build a Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

Today’s challenge focused on designing an interactive simulator to experience the hospital admission process as a structured healthcare workflow.

Rather than simply documenting the process, the simulator allows users to understand how different steps, decisions, and dependencies come together before a patient is ready for admission.

🔑 Key Learning
• Breaking complex healthcare processes into structured workflows
• Understanding dependencies between intake, documentation, verification, and readiness
• Using AI to create realistic, scenario-based learning experiences
• Designing interactive workflows that support better process understanding

💡 Key Benefits
• Improves healthcare workflow awareness
• Enables safe practice without impacting real patients
• Helps identify process gaps and potential bottlenecks
• Makes training more engaging and practical
• Supports consistent understanding of admission procedures

Key takeaway: AI can go beyond answering questions—it can create interactive simulations that help people learn, practice, and understand complex real-world workflows.
