
For Day 34 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I built The AI Marketing Detective—an interactive investigative engine designed to debug marketing funnels, analyze campaign drop-offs, and solve conversion anomalies using root-cause analysis.

Most marketing reports stop at "what" happened (e.g., "CPAs increased by 40%"). True growth strategists focus on "why". Instead of relying on superficial vanity metrics, this tool prompts users through a structured forensic investigation to isolate campaign culprits—from broken attribution pixels to hidden audience fatigue.

Using Claude, I engineered a detective simulation where users investigate data trails to solve real marketing mysteries.

🔍 How the Marketing Detective Framework Works:
Anomaly Detection & Baseline Audit:

Scans campaign data to identify statistical outliers—such as sudden conversion rate dips, traffic quality spikes, or CPC jumps.

Forensic Funnel Analysis:

Traces the user journey step-by-step (Ad Impression → Landing Page → Checkout → Retargeting) to locate exact drop-off points.

Root-Cause Hypothesis Testing:

Evaluates technical, creative, and external variables: tracking script failures, ad platform algorithm updates, landing page load latency, or competitive ad surges.

Actionable Remediation Playbook:

Generates prioritized corrective steps—from audience refresh strategies to conversion rate optimization (CRO) hotfixes.

💡 Core Strategic Benefits & Key Learnings:
🎯 Beyond Vanity Metrics: Teaches teams to look past high impressions and focus on metrics that directly impact customer acquisition costs (CAC) and ROI.

🛠️ Faster Funnel Debugging: Reduces the time spent manually cross-referencing analytics tools by automating pattern recognition across paid, organic, and web channels.

🧠 Structured Problem-Solving: Shifts marketing ops from emotional guesswork to a systematic, hypothesis-driven investigative approach.

The Key Takeaway:
Data doesn't lie, but it often hides the truth. Treating performance drops like investigative puzzles turns raw analytics into a clear roadmap for growth.
