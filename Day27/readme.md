For Day 27 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I took yesterday’s Prior Authorization simulator a step further by building a Prior Authorization Story Simulator—using narrative-driven, conversational AI to teach complex healthcare operations.

Process charts and compliance PDFs tell you what the rules are, but story-driven simulations show you how decisions ripple through an ecosystem. By turning clinical documentation and payer friction into an interactive narrative, complex healthcare workflows become intuitive, memorable, and human-centered.

Here is how I structured the story simulator in Claude:

📖 1. Multi-Perspective Narrative Engine

Immersed the user in dynamic branching storylines, letting them navigate scenarios from the perspective of a clinical coordinator, a payer medical director, or a patient advocate.

🗣️ 2. Conversational Negotiations & Peer-to-Peer Dialogue

Built realistic dialogue trees where users hold simulated phone calls and peer-to-peer discussions to defend clinical necessity using ICD-10/CPT guidelines.

🔀 3. Consequence-Driven Branching Logic

Every decision directly impacts outcomes—leading to instant treatment approvals, delayed care cycles, administrative appeals, or unexpected out-of-pocket costs.

📊 4. Narrative Debrief & Compliance Scorecard

Concludes each story arc with an executive debrief: analyzing clinical documentation quality, communication empathy, and process efficiency.

💡 The Key Takeaway:
When training teams on complex, highly regulated processes, narrative context beats static documentation every time. Conversational story simulators transform dry policy manuals into immersive, risk-free learning environments.
