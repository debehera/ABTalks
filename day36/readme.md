
🚀 #60DayClaudeChallenge – Day 36

🧠 Explore Your Thinking Patterns

Today’s challenge focused on understanding how we naturally approach decisions, uncertainty, and problem-solving through an interactive AI experience.

🔑 Key Learning
• Recognize different approaches to decision-making
• Understand how we respond to uncertainty and incomplete information
• Identify patterns in our reasoning and problem-solving
• Reflect on assumptions, biases, and decision habits
• Use AI as a tool for structured self-reflection

💡 Key Benefits
• Builds greater awareness of personal decision-making patterns
• Encourages more deliberate and structured thinking
• Helps identify potential blind spots
• Supports better reflection when dealing with uncertainty
• Demonstrates how AI can create engaging self-assessment experiences

Key takeaway: Better decisions start with understanding how we think. AI can help us reflect on our reasoning—but the goal is greater awareness, not replacing human judgment.
