                    Data sources & methodology

                    Live AQI (US scale), PM2.5 and PM10 readings for Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Jaipur were pulled from aqi.in and the aqicn.org real-time monitoring network on Jul 25, 2026. AQI for Pali, Buxar, Jaunpur, Bhubaneswar, Varanasi and Anantapur is estimated from aqi.in's "Most Polluted Cities" live ranking (category + multiple-of-standard indicator, since exact figures weren't published) — flagged "estimated" throughout. PM2.5/PM10 for those six cities is derived using the standard US EPA AQI-to-concentration breakpoint formula plus a typical Indian urban PM10:PM2.5 ratio. Cities are categorized using the Good/Satisfactory/Moderate/Poor/Very Poor/Severe scale. Water Quality Scores are modeled from the Bureau of Indian Standards' 2019 tap-water testing study (21 state capitals) and CPCB/NITI Aayog composite water-management trends — treat these as directional, not lab-grade per-city readings. Environmental Health, Air, Water, Hair-risk and Skin-risk scores are Claude-computed composite indices, not clinical measurements.
