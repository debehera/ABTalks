
🚀 #60DayClaudeChallenge – Day 30

📦 Build a Supply Chain Optimizer

Today’s challenge focused on using AI to analyze and optimize an existing supply chain from an operations leader’s perspective.

The goal was to balance inventory, demand, suppliers, lead times, costs, and operational risks while making data-driven decisions.

🔑 Key Learning
• How AI can support complex operational decision-making
• Identifying bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and supply risks
• Balancing cost optimization with service levels
• Turning operational data into actionable insights

💡 Key Benefits
• Better inventory and demand planning
• Reduced operational costs and waste
• Improved supply chain visibility
• Faster, data-driven decision-making
• Early identification of potential disruptions

Key takeaway: AI can move beyond automation—it can act as a decision-support partner for operations leaders, helping optimize complex business processes.

Another great learning experience from the #60DayClaudeChallenge! 🚀
