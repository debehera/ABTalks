
For Day 22 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I used Claude to stress-test a common startup idea through a venture capitalist lens: an "AI Assistant for Company Documentation."

The pitch: An enterprise RAG system that lets employees query SOPs, HR policies, and technical guides using natural language instead of hunting through drive folders.

Sound like a winning idea? A VC doesn't buy the hype—they stress-test the assumptions.

Here is how I used Claude to break down the idea into evidence-based insights:

🎯 1. Moat & Defensibility Audit

The Risk: RAG-over-docs is an easy wrapper to build. How do you defend against enterprise search tools like Microsoft Copilot or Notion AI?

The Insight: The true moat isn't the AI search—it's granular permission mapping (RBAC), zero-data retention guarantees, and live sync pipelines with legacy enterprise systems.

⚡ 2. Unit Economics & Cost Scaling

The Risk: High vector database hosting and chunking/embedding costs eat margins as doc sizes explode.

The Insight: Monetization must scale on active query volume and storage tiers, not just per-seat pricing.

🛡️ 3. Security & Compliance Friction

The Risk: Enterprise CISOs reject third-party tools that process sensitive internal IP without compliance guarantees.

The Insight: A successful MVP must prioritize on-prem/private cloud deployment models and SOC2 readiness over flashy chat UI features.

💡 The Key Takeaway:
Generative AI makes building easy, but building the right business model hard. Using AI to simulate VC pushback forces you to stop pitching features and start validating moat, margins, and market friction.
