
🚀 #60DayClaudeChallenge – Day 33

📰 Build a Media Integrity Analyzer

Today’s challenge focused on an important AI skill: evaluating information before believing or sharing it.

The goal was to build an analyzer that helps examine content, identify potential red flags, separate facts from opinions, and encourage more critical thinking.

🔑 Key Learning
• Evaluate information critically rather than accepting it at face value
• Identify potential bias, misleading claims, and missing context
• Distinguish facts, opinions, assumptions, and interpretations
• Use structured analysis to assess information quality
• Understand the importance of verification and reliable sources

💡 Key Benefits
• Promotes responsible information consumption
• Helps reduce the risk of spreading misinformation
• Encourages evidence-based thinking
• Improves digital and AI literacy
• Builds better habits for evaluating online content

Key takeaway: In an AI-powered world, the ability to question, verify, and critically evaluate information is becoming just as important as the ability to generate it.
