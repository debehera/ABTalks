Disclaimer & Nutrition Sources

Please read before relying on any figures in this tool.

NutriScope is an educational and self-tracking tool only. It is not a medical device, and its output does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Calorie, macro, and micronutrient figures are population-level estimates derived from standard formulas (e.g. Mifflin–St Jeor) and generalized food composition data — they will not exactly match your individual physiology, lab values, or specific health conditions.

The Risk Analysis section describes general associations reported in public health literature between nutrient intake patterns and common conditions. It is not a screening tool and cannot detect or rule out any disease.

If you have a diagnosed medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are managing a chronic illness, or are considering a significant change to your diet, please consult a physician or a registered dietitian for guidance tailored to you.

Nutrition Data Sources
The nutrient values and daily reference intakes in this app are approximated from the following types of public reference sources.
USDA FoodData Central
U.S. Department of Agriculture's food composition database, used as a general reference for macro and micronutrient values per 100g.
ICMR – National Institute of Nutrition (India)
Dietary Guidelines for Indians and the Indian Food Composition Tables, referenced for regionally common foods.
WHO / FAO Nutrient Reference Values
General guidance on macronutrient distribution ranges and micronutrient intake recommendations for adults.
NIH Office of Dietary Supplements
General fact sheets on vitamins and minerals, used to inform the Risk Analysis associations shown in this app.
