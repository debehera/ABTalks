
For Day 26 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I used Claude to build a Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator—converting one of healthcare’s most notorious administrative bottlenecks into an interactive, decision-driven experience.

Prior authorization (PA) in healthcare is notoriously complex—involving strict payer rules, clinical guidelines (ICD-10/CPT codes), medical necessity documentation, and peer-to-peer appeals. By turning these heavy operational workflows into a dynamic simulation, you can train teams, test process efficiency, and identify friction points without operational risk.

Here is how I structured the workflow simulator inside Claude:

📋 1. Clinical Patient & Payer Scenario Generation

Configured Claude to generate randomized, realistic patient cases with complex medical histories, insurance plan constraints, and target treatment/procedure requests.

⚡ 2. Interactive Decision Engine & Compliance Checks

Simulated real-time interactions where the user plays the role of a healthcare provider or authorization specialist—submitting clinical rationale, attaching documentation, and navigating payer approval rules.

🔄 3. Appeals, Peer-to-Peer & Peer Review Branching

Built dynamic branching paths for initial denials—requiring users to draft medical necessity appeals, review peer-to-peer trial notes, and cite clinical guidelines to overturn rejections.

📊 4. Operational Performance Dashboard

Evaluates completed cases with key operational metrics: Approval Velocity, Denial Rate Reduction, Clinical Documentation Accuracy, and Administrative Cost Impact.

💡 The Key Takeaway:
Interactive simulation is the ultimate bridge between complex enterprise domain rules and user adoption. Whether in healthcare, finance, or cloud architecture, turning static workflows into playable AI simulations accelerates learning and highlights process bottlenecks instantly.
